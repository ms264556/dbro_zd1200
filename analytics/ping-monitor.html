What the history shows

Blue bar
Mean latency; its dark line is the maximum.
Red bar
At least one ping was lost. Dark red means every ping was lost.
Grey
No monitoring data; never interpreted as healthy.
Amber SNR
40 dB or stronger is a flat line. Weaker SNR extends downward from 40 dB; the dark mark is the lowest observation.
Amber strip
AP airtime on the selected band, in a separate strip below SNR. Hover for the percentage.
A / B
Moments selected for configuration analysis.
